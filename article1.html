Installation Guide

Learn how to download and install the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience on your Windows device.

How to Access the CANDU Virtual Reality Experience

To access the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience, click on the get started button on the homepage. A dropdown menu will appear asking if you are a student, parent, or educator. Choose the option that best describes you. An executable file will then automatically download - click on it to open it up.

A popup window stating "Windows protected your PC" will appear. Simply click the "more info" text, and then the "run anyway" option. Another popup will appear asking if you give permission to allow this program to make changes to your PC - click the "yes" option.

The installation setup will then appear. Simply click the "next" and "install" buttons and wait for the program to install. Once done, make sure the launch option is checked, and click "finish". The CANDU Virtual Reality Educational program is now installed on your computer and will launch. These steps are showcased in the video below.
