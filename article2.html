Navigation Control Guide

Master the controls and navigation system of the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience.

Navigate in the CANDU Virtual Reality Experience

This section explains how to navigate in the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational experience fuel channel tutorial room upon entering. You will navigate by using the WASD keys and your mouse or touchpad if you are not using a virtual reality headset.

Basic Movement Controls

  • W Key: Move forward
  • A Key: Move left
  • S Key: Move backward
  • D Key: Move right
  • Mouse/Touchpad: Look around the room

Interaction Controls

  • Left/Right Click: Click and hold to pick up objects
  • T Key: Hold and aim at teleportation targets on the ground to teleport between rooms

Calandria Viewer Room Navigation

This section explains how to navigate in the CANDU Calandria viewer room. You will navigate the same way as in the fuel channel room, using the WASD keys and your mouse or touchpad.

Exploring the Calandria

  • W Key: Walk slowly towards the full-scale calandria
  • S Key: Move backwards to get a view of the outer components
  • A/D Keys: Move left and right around the calandria
  • Mouse Movement: Look around to examine different angles

Interactive Features

To activate the color coding feature, use your cursor to touch the different calandria components. This will help you identify and learn about various parts of the reactor system, including:

  • Moderator components
  • Fuel channels
  • Control mechanisms
  • Coolant systems

Navigation Tips

Best Practices

  • Take Your Time: Move slowly to fully appreciate the detail in each component
  • Experiment with Angles: Use the mouse to look up, down, and around components
  • Use Teleportation: Look for teleportation targets to quickly move between areas
  • Interactive Elements: Click on components to learn more about their function

VR Headset Users

If you're using a VR headset, the controls may vary depending on your specific device. Consult your VR headset's documentation for movement and interaction controls.
