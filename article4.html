How to Make the Most Out of Your Learning Experience

To maximize your learning experience with the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience, follow these best practices and preparation steps:

Before You Start

Spend some time reading the articles and watching the videos on the CANDU website. Start with these essential guides:

  • Installation Guide: "How to access the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience"
  • Navigation Guide: "How to navigate in the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience"

These articles will teach you how to successfully install, access, navigate, and interact with the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience software.

Educational Preparation

Watch the educational videos on the CANDU website homepage, especially the "CANDU Calandria Reactor" video. This will provide you with the foundational knowledge needed to understand CANDU reactor components and successfully complete the CANDU quiz.

During Your VR Experience

  • Listen carefully to voice instructions and read all dialog boxes
  • Take your time to explore each virtual environment thoroughly
  • Use the teleportation feature to access different viewpoints
  • Interact with components to activate color coding and learn about different parts

Navigation Tips

Quick Exit: To escape from any virtual room and return to the menu, press the Esc and Shift keys simultaneously on your keyboard.
