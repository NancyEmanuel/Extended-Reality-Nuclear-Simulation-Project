How to Make the Most Out of Your Learning Experience

To maximize your learning experience with the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience, it's important to prepare properly and follow these best practices:

Preparation Phase

  1. Read the Documentation: Start by reading the instruction articles on "How to Access the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience" and "How to Navigate in the CANDU Virtual Reality Educational Experience"
  2. Watch Preparatory Videos: View the educational videos on the homepage, particularly the "CANDU Calandria Reactor" video to understand reactor components
  3. Review Nuclear Concepts: Familiarize yourself with basic nuclear physics and reactor principles before entering the VR environment

During Your VR Experience

  1. Listen to Voice Instructions: Pay careful attention to the voice guidance provided throughout the experience
  2. Read Dialog Boxes: Take time to read all dialog and information panels to understand the tasks you should attempt
  3. Interact with Components: Click on different reactor components to learn about their functions and properties
  4. Take Your Time: Don't rush through the experience - explore each area thoroughly

Navigation Tips

  • Emergency Exit: Press Escape (Esc) + Shift keys simultaneously to return to the menu from any virtual room
  • Movement Controls: Use WASD keys for movement and mouse for looking around
  • Teleportation: Use the T key to teleport between designated areas

Effective Study Strategies

Before Using VR

  • Set specific learning objectives for each session
  • Review relevant nuclear engineering concepts
  • Prepare questions you want to explore in the VR environment
  • Ensure you have adequate time for a complete session

During VR Sessions

  • Take notes on key observations and learnings
  • Experiment with different viewing angles of components
  • Complete all interactive elements and quizzes
  • Revisit complex areas multiple times for better understanding

After VR Sessions

  • Review your notes and consolidate key learnings
  • Research additional information about components you found interesting
  • Discuss your experience with peers or instructors
  • Plan your next VR session based on areas needing more exploration

Quiz Preparation

The CANDU VR experience includes assessment components to test your understanding. Here's how to prepare:

Key Topics to Master

  • Reactor Components: Understand the function of each major component
  • Nuclear Processes: Grasp the fundamentals of nuclear fission and heat generation
  • Safety Systems: Learn about the various safety mechanisms in place
  • Coolant Systems: Understand how heat is transferred and managed
  • Control Systems: Learn how the reactor is controlled and monitored

Study Resources

  • Interactive VR component labels and descriptions
  • Voice narration throughout the VR experience
