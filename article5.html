Learn About VR Education

Virtual Reality is a computer-generated simulated experience of a three-dimensional environment with visual and environmental stimuli. Virtual Reality isolates the user from the real world to create an immersion effect that gives the user the feeling of being present in a virtual world.

VR in Nuclear Education

This video explains what Virtual Reality is and how it is being used to modernize the education and training process regarding CANDU reactors. The video goes into depth about Virtual Reality technology and how it can benefit students looking to learn more about CANDU Reactors.

Benefits of VR Learning

  • Immersive Experience: Students can explore reactor components in a safe, controlled virtual environment
  • Interactive Learning: Hands-on interaction with complex nuclear systems
  • Accessibility: Remote access to expensive and restricted nuclear facilities
  • Safety: Learn about dangerous processes without physical risk
  • Repeatability: Practice procedures multiple times for better retention
