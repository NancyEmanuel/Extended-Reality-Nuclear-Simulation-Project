CANDU Calandria Reactors

View Video Transcript

Video Transcript: This educational video explains the fundamental principles of CANDU reactors, including how nuclear fission generates heat, how heavy water moderates neutrons, and how steam generation produces electricity. The video covers key components like fuel channels, calandria, and safety systems.

CANDU Reactors are Canadian-designed heavy water reactors that represent a pinnacle of nuclear engineering. These reactors utilize natural uranium fuel and heavy water (deuterium oxide) as both moderator and coolant, creating a highly efficient and safe nuclear power generation system.

The process begins with natural uranium converted into fuel pellets, where nuclear fission splits uranium atoms to generate heat. The heavy water moderator slows down neutrons, increasing the probability of fission reactions. This heat is then transferred to steam generators, converting regular water into pressurized steam that drives turbines to generate electricity.
